Intelligent Planning Tools

Every feature designed to help you capture the cosmos

FoV Framing Intelligence

Input your sensor size and focal length. Our algorithm scores and ranks targets based on how perfectly they'll frame in your specific equipment setup.

Advanced Multi-Filter System

Filter by altitude, angular size, moon separation, object type, and sky quadrant. Find the perfect target for your conditions instantly.

Real-Time Celestial Mechanics

See altitude/azimuth paths, transit times, and visibility windows. Powered by the Astronomy Engine for sub-arcminute accuracy—completely offline.

AR Horizon Scanner

Map your local horizon with AR. Scan trees, buildings, and terrain to see exactly when targets will clear obstructions at your shooting location.

Landscape Alignment Calculator

Pin a foreground object, select a celestial target and time. We calculate the exact GPS coordinates where you need to stand for perfect alignment.

Astronomy-Specific Weather

Integration with Astrospheric API provides seeing, transparency, and cloud cover data—the metrics that actually matter for astrophotography.

Light Pollution Mapping

Custom backend service samples real light pollution data to show Bortle class and sky quality for any location on Earth.

Personal Target Management

Track captured objects and maintain a priority wishlist. The app adapts to hide completed targets and highlight your personal goals.
